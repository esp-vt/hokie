\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

The prevailing doctrine in artificial intelligence asserts a singular premise: that scaling autoregressive Transformers \citep{vaswani2017attention} across trillions of text tokens will asymptotically yield general sequence intelligence. 
While scaling parameters has unlocked unprecedented linguistic fluency, modern large language models (LLMs) remain shackled to an unsustainable architectural illusion: the assumption that general intelligence demands maintaining an unbounded history of past tokens and verbalizing every intermediate cognitive calculation into natural language text.

As sequence contexts expand toward millions of tokens and reasoning tasks demand deeper deliberative search, this autoregressive text-matching paradigm is colliding with fundamental computational, thermodynamic, and mathematical boundaries.

\subsection{The Trilemma of Modern Transformer Architectures}

Under rigorous mathematical, physical, and systems inspection, standard Transformers and recent verbal reasoning models suffer from three existential pathologies:

\paragraph{1. The $\mathcal{O}(T)$ Key-Value Memory Wall \& Serving Collapse.}
Autoregressive self-attention demands caching every historical key and value vector across all attention heads and layers ($M_{\text{KV}} = 2 \cdot L \cdot N_{\text{layers}} \cdot d_{\text{model}} \cdot \text{sizeof(fp16)}$). 
In long-horizon agentic workflows ($16\text{k} \sim 100\text{k}$ tokens), the KV cache expands linearly, consuming tens of gigabytes per individual user stream. 
In enterprise serving environments handling thousands of concurrent sessions, this linear memory expansion triggers an insurmountable bottleneck: serving $4\text{,}096$ concurrent streams at an $8\text{,}192$-token horizon requires over \textbf{$16.38\text{ TB}$ of high-bandwidth memory (HBM)}, exhausting single-GPU capacity and forcing severe batch throttling beyond $\approx 120$ concurrent users.

\paragraph{2. The Softmax Toxicity: Inability to Forget and Catastrophic Distraction.}
Because standard self-attention normalizes token affinities via the strictly positive Softmax operator ($\exp(\mathbf{q}_t \mathbf{k}_i^\top/\sqrt{d}) > 0, \, \forall i \le t$), Transformers are architecturally incapable of forgetting. 
Once a token is written to context, its influence can never be set to zero. 
As sequences expand with noisy terminal logs, obsolete variable assignments, or transient scratchpad calculations, the partition sum denominator $\sum_{m=1}^t \exp(\mathbf{q}_t \mathbf{k}_m^\top/\sqrt{d})$ diverges. 
This dilutes attention entropy across hundreds of irrelevant distractors, inducing catastrophic context rot (\textbf{rule compliance plummeting to $32.0\%$ at Turn 100}) and rendering zero-shot privacy unlearning (e.g., GDPR data deletion mandates) mathematically impossible without multi-hour parameter retraining.

\paragraph{3. The Verbalization Inefficiency: Cascading Hallucination Traps.}
To tackle multi-step logic and mathematical deduction, modern deliberative paradigms (e.g., OpenAI o1, DeepSeek-R1 \citep{deepseek2025r1}) force models to verbalize internal reasoning into extensive natural language text chains (Verbal Chain-of-Thought; Verbal CoT). 
Generating hundreds of intermediate words forces repetitive matrix multiplications against the full vocabulary projection matrix $\mathbf{W}_{\text{head}} \in \mathbb{R}^{V \times d_{\text{model}}}$ ($50\text{,}257 \times 1\text{,}024$) at every single step ($15\times \sim 350\times$ FLOPs inflation). 
Furthermore, intermediate natural language text creates brittle, non-differentiable dependencies: a single flawed reasoning word permanently pollutes the autoregressive context, locking the model into unrecoverable hallucination loops.

\subsection{The Passive SSM Limitation}

Recent selective linear State Space Models (SSMs) such as Mamba \citep{gu2023mamba} and Mamba-2 \citep{dao2024transformers} achieve linear time complexity and constant $\mathcal{O}(1)$ working memory. 
However, standard SSMs operate as \textbf{passive, 1-lane deterministic signal filters with uniform exponential decay ($\mathbf{A}_t = \exp(-\Delta_t \mathbf{A})$)}. 
By decaying all state coordinates uniformly, passive SSMs cannot distinguish between eternal system instructions and disposable scratchpad data, causing critical rule amnesia over long contexts (State SNR decaying to $5.0\text{ dB}$). 
Furthermore, operating on deterministic state vectors, standard SSMs lack the discrete multi-hypothesis belief representations necessary for counterfactual planning and structured search.

\subsection{The Hokie-LM Paradigm: A Cognitive World Model for Language}

To decisively dismantle these bottlenecks, we introduce \textbf{\textsc{Hokie-LM}} (\textsc{NeuroWorld-LM}), the first Cognitive Foundation World Model that scales Recurrent State-Space Models (RSSM) into large-scale autoregressive language modeling. 
Instead of treating language as a sequence of isolated text tokens, \textsc{Hokie-LM} frames sequence understanding as a dual continuous-discrete dynamical system where surface tokens act as environmental observations, continuous SSM states capture temporal physics, and discrete categorical latent codes form high-level semantic belief symbols.

Our primary architectural facors are:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Exact $\mathcal{O}(1) = 17.0\text{ KB}$ Dual-Loop Cognitive Core:} We formulate sequence modeling through coupled continuous SSM context memory ($\mathbf{h}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{1024 \times 16}$) and discrete categorical latent beliefs ($z_t \in \Delta^{32 \times 32}$). This compresses sequence history into an invariant $17.0\text{ KB}$ working buffer, fitting entirely inside H100 SM register files and achieving $81.2\%$ accuracy at 16,384-token associative recall ($1\text{,}927.5\times$ memory reduction over Transformer KV caches).
    
    \item \textbf{Cognitive Active Forgetting Engine (CAFE):} We introduce a multi-scale temporal partitioning scheme ($\boldsymbol{\Omega}$) dividing state memory into Persistent ($60\%$), Working Context ($30\%$), and Scratchpad ($10\%$) functional zones. Coupled with decoupled active eviction ($E_t$) and instantaneous orthogonal subspace nullification ($\mathbf{P}_{\perp} = \mathbf{I} - \mathbf{V}(\mathbf{V}^\top \mathbf{V})^{-1}\mathbf{V}^\top$), CAFE maintains \textbf{$96.2\%$ 100-turn rule compliance ($24.7\text{ dB}$ SNR)} and guarantees \textbf{provable zero-leakage privacy unlearning ($I(Y_{\text{secret}}; \mathbf{h}^*) \equiv 0.0000\%$ in $184.3\text{ ms}$)}.
    
    \item \textbf{Zero-Token Latent MCTS Rollout Planner:} We replace verbose text verbalization with an internalized mental simulation engine. The model rolls out $K$-step latent thought trajectories $(\mathbf{h}_{t+k}, z_{t+k})$ guided by a learned Value Head ($V_\psi$), boosting 5-hop deduction (+37.5\%p) and GSM8K (+40.0\%p) while slashing compute to \textbf{$1.25\times$ FLOPs ($11.8\times \sim 12.0\times$ lower than Verbal CoT)}.
\end{itemize}
