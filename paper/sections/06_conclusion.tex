\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we introduced \textbf{Hokie-LM}, a foundation architecture that re-imagines language modeling as a generative world model with dual continuous-discrete state space dynamics and intelligent active forgetting.
By combining selective state-space memory with discrete categorical latent beliefs, Hokie-LM eliminates the $\mathcal{O}(T)$ KV cache memory wall, achieving constant $17.0\text{ KB}$ inference memory across 32k+ token horizons ($1\text{,}927.5\times$ compression).
Furthermore, by introducing the Zero-Token Latent Rollout Planner with surprise-driven gating, the model performs internal mental simulations in latent space, achieving substantial accuracy jumps on multi-hop deductive logic and GSM8K mathematical reasoning while reducing computational FLOPs by up to $11.8\times \sim 12\times$ compared to Verbal Chain-of-Thought ($1.25\times$ vs $14.8\times$).
Through the Cognitive Active Forgetting Engine (CAFE), Hokie-LM preserves pristine memory states (+58.8\%p on CD-NIAH, 99.4\% accuracy) and guarantees mathematical zero-leakage unlearning under adversarial red-teaming.
Our theoretical analysis guarantees the global stability of ungrounded deep rollouts and proves online regret minimization.
We believe Hokie-LM opens a promising avenue toward scalable, constant-memory, and deliberative language intelligence.
